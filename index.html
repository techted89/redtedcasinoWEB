Red Ted's Dice!

A simplified version of the classic casino game, Craps.

First Roll:
- Roll a 7 or 11, you win!
- Roll a 2, 3, or 12, you lose.
- Any other number becomes the 'Point'.
Subsequent Rolls:
- Roll the 'Point' again, you win!
- Roll a 7, you lose.
